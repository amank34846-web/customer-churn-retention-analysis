# Business Insights & Recommendations

## Overall Performance
The dataset contains 1,500 customers. 66 are marked as churned, giving an overall churn rate of 4.40%.

## Contract Risk
Monthly has the highest observed churn rate (5.42%).

## Plan Risk
Premium has the highest observed churn rate (5.61%).

## Regional Risk
North has the highest observed churn rate (6.07%).

## Recommendations
- Prioritize retention efforts for high-churn contract and plan segments.
- Identify customers with low satisfaction and repeated support interactions.
- Review onboarding and engagement for newer customers if early-tenure churn is elevated.
- Investigate payment-related friction where late payments are associated with churn.
- Track churn rate, retained customers, and customer value before and after retention campaigns.

These findings describe patterns in a synthetic dataset and do not establish causation for a real business.
